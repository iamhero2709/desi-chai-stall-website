Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Desi Chai Stall Sort (Android package com.desichai.stallsort) is provided by zeroshotlab. Contact: hello@zeroshotlab.in.

1. Game progress and notifications

Levels, coins, boosters, settings, purchases and daily rewards are saved on your device. The game does not require an account, offer a social feed, or upload your progress to a developer-operated game server. Local data remains until app storage is cleared or the app is removed, subject to Android backup and restore settings. Optional reminders are scheduled on your device after notification permission is granted. We do not register a remote push token or run a push campaign service.

2. Advertising through Google AdMob

The game uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information. These support advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Processing depends on your region, consent choices and device settings. Ad-related processing can occur even for non-personalized or test ads.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads are requested. Available consent controls can be reopened in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Advertising identifiers can also be reset or deleted through Android settings. Remove Ads and an active Chai Pass disable banners and automatic interstitial/app-open ads; optional rewarded ads remain available when you choose them.

Google privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google partner data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Google Play Billing and RevenueCat

Optional purchases include coin and booster packs, a starter pack, a piggy bank, Remove Ads and Chai Pass. Google Play processes payments. We do not receive your full card or bank details. RevenueCat validates transactions, manages entitlements and restores eligible purchases. It processes an anonymous app-specific customer identifier, purchase history and tokens, product identifiers, and relevant device, app and store information for app functionality, purchase analytics and fraud prevention.

Chai Pass is an optional monthly auto-renewing subscription. The store shows the price, billing period and any applicable terms before purchase. Manage or cancel it through Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancellation stops future renewals; access generally continues for the paid period, subject to Google's refund policies. Consumable items already spent are not restored on a new installation. Restore purchases is available in Settings for eligible purchases.

RevenueCat privacy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/
Manage subscriptions: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

4. Analytics and diagnostics

This version does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or a separate remote gameplay analytics service. Gameplay event logging is local console logging. AdMob and RevenueCat still process the service-related analytics and diagnostics described above.

5. Support and your choices

If you email hello@zeroshotlab.in, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond. Please do not send passwords or full payment details. To request access, correction or deletion of information held by us or our purchase provider, contact that address with enough information to locate your record. We may request minimal verification. Local game data can be removed in Android app settings. Removing local data does not cancel a subscription or erase Google's payment or advertising records; use Google's controls for those records.

6. Retention, providers and security

Support messages are kept while needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Advertising and purchase providers retain records under their policies; some purchase records may be needed for restoration, fraud prevention or legal requirements. Providers may process data outside your country. Their SDK communications use supported secure transport, including HTTPS/TLS. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed completely secure. We do not sell support messages or local game-progress records. Ad-related collection and sharing are described above.

7. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask players to submit a personal profile. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact hello@zeroshotlab.in so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Website and changes

Our public policy is available on Google Sites, which may process website visitor information under Google's privacy policy. Our developer website is hosted on Vercel, which may process routine request and security logs under its own privacy policy: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy. We may update this policy when the game or its services change and will update the date above.
